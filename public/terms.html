the terms, in plain words.

Churpie is a free way to gather short video messages for someone and turn them into one reel. Using it means you agree to what's on this page. It's short on purpose.

who it's for

Churpie is for people 13 and older. Younger kids are welcome in a message that a parent or another grown-up records with them.

what you put in a card

  • Your message stays yours. You're letting us store it, put it in the reel with everyone else's, and show it to the host and the person the card is for. That's all we do with it.
  • Only record or upload people who know they're being recorded and are fine with it.
  • Nothing illegal, hateful, sexual or threatening, and nothing meant to embarrass or hurt someone. Don't use music, film or other work that isn't yours to share.
  • The host decides what goes in the reel and can hide any message. We can remove a message or a whole card that breaks these rules.

links are the keys

There are no accounts. Whoever has a card's private link can use it, so share the invite with the group and keep the host's card link to yourself. If you lose yours, find your card.

we delete things

Churpie isn't a place to store videos. The reel and every message are deleted 14 days after the reel is made, so save the reel if you want to keep it. The details are on the privacy page. Once something is deleted, we can't bring it back.

as it is

We work hard to make every reel come out right, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes. Save your reel as soon as it's ready. To the extent the law allows, Sherwood Digital isn't responsible for losses that come from using churpie, including a reel that fails or is deleted on schedule.
