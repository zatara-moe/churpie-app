Questions

Questions people ask

Is churpie really free?

Yes. Churpie is free for people, always: no ads, no per-card fees, no trial that runs out.

Does anyone need an app or an account?

No. Everyone taps one link and records in their phone's browser, uses their phone's own camera, or writes a short note instead. Nobody makes an account. You just give your email so we can send you your card page and the finished reel.

How many people can be in one card?

Up to 50 people. Each one adds a message of about 15 seconds, or 30 if you choose.

What occasions is it for?

Birthdays, graduations, retirements, get well soon, welcome home, thank yous, team send-offs and memorials. Each card has a look and music that suits the moment.

Can I check the messages before they see it?

Yes. You get a private card page where every message lands first. Watch them, change the order, and hide any you like. Nothing reaches them until you send it.

How long do you keep the videos?

We delete the reel and every message 14 days after the reel is made, so save it if you want to keep it. A card nobody adds to for 30 days is deleted 14 days later.

Who makes churpie?

Churpie is made by Sherwood Digital in Flagstaff, Arizona.
