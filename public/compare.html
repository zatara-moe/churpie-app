churpie vs. the paid group video apps.

VidDay and Tribute are good at what they do: long videos, photos and printed video books, and Tribute offers a professional editor, for a price. Churpie does one thing: a short reel from everyone who loves someone, free, with no account for anyone. Here’s the honest difference.

pick churpie if…

  • You want it free, for you and everyone in it.
  • Nobody should have to make an account, including you.
  • A short reel of faces and voices is the gift, not an hour-long film.

pick a paid app if…

  • You want a long video, photos mixed in, or (with Tribute) a professional editor.
  • You want a printed keepsake, like a video book.
  • You want the video hosted for you indefinitely, instead of saving your own copy.

questions people ask.

Is churpie really free?

Yes. Churpie is free for people, always: no ads, no per-card fees, no trial that runs out. It’s made by Sherwood Digital in Flagstaff, Arizona.

What does churpie not do?

No photo slideshows, long films, editing services or printed keepsakes. It makes one short reel from everyone’s messages, and deletes it 14 days after it’s made.

Does anyone need an app or an account?

Not for churpie: not the people adding messages, not the person organizing it. Tribute says its guests don’t need an app; check VidDay’s and Tribute’s sites for their account rules.
