privacy, in plain words.

Churpie makes a video card from everyone who loves someone. To do that, we keep a few things for a short time, and then we delete them. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is sold to anyone.

what we keep

  • From the host: their name, their email, the name of the person the card is for, the question everyone answers, any house rules, and an optional last day. The email is used only to send the link to their card, the finished reel, and one reminder before it's deleted. No newsletter, and it's never shared.
  • From each contributor: their name, where they are (if they add it), and their video or written note.
  • On your own phone or computer: links to the cards you've made, so the home page can show "your cards," and whether you've seen the recording tips. That stays on your device and you can remove it there.
  • The usual web basics: like any website, the service that runs churpie (Cloudflare) handles your connection, which includes your IP address. We also use Cloudflare's privacy-first visit counter to see how many people visit and how fast pages load. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites. There are no ads and no advertising trackers on churpie.

who can see it

Each card has three private links: the invite (for the people adding messages), the host's card page, and the reel (for the person it's for). They're long and hard to guess, and we keep them out of search engines. Anyone who has a link can open it, so share them the way you'd share a private photo album.

The host can watch every message, change the order, and hide any of them before the reel is made. Hidden messages never go in the reel.

when it's deleted

  • The original videos are deleted once the reel has been sent, or 3 days after it's made, whichever comes first.
  • The reel and every message are deleted 14 days after the reel is made. The host can add 14 more days, once. Save the reel if you want to keep it.
  • A card nobody adds to for 30 days is marked for deletion and deleted 14 days later, unless the host keeps it open.
  • When a card ends (or the host deletes it), everything goes: every video, written note and name, the question, and the host's email. Only an anonymous count that a card existed is kept.
  • Contributors can replace or remove their own message any time before the reel is made, using the private link they got after sending it.

kids

Churpie is made for people 13 and older. Younger kids are welcome to appear in a message that a parent or another grown-up records with them.
