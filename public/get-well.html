how it works.

  1. Ask one gentle questionTheir name and one thing everyone answers. About a minute.
  2. Send one linkTo family, friends, the team. No sign-in needed, and nobody needs to visit.
  3. Send it when they’re readyHide anything that feels like too much, then send them the reel.

questions that feel right.

  • “One thing you can’t wait to do together when they’re back.”
  • “Tell them about a time they made you laugh.”
  • “What are you doing for them while they rest?”
  • “Say what you’d say if you were sitting next to them.”

keep it gentle.

  • Pick the Quiet Note card. It sets soft music and a calm look.
  • Ask people to keep it light and short. Fifteen seconds is plenty.
  • You see every message first. Hide anything that might be hard to hear.

questions people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Churpie is free for people, always: no ads, no per-card fees, no trial that runs out.

Can they watch it in the hospital?

Yes. It plays on any phone, tablet or laptop. They can pause, skip, and come back to it any time.

Can someone write instead of recording?

Yes. Written notes show up in the reel as a postcard with their name.

How long can they keep it?

The reel is theirs to save and keep. We delete our copy, and every message, 14 days after the reel is made.
