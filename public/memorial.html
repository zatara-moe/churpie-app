how it works.

  1. Ask one questionTheir name and one thing everyone shares. About a minute.
  2. Send one linkTo family, friends and anyone who knew them. No app, no sign-up.
  3. Share it with the familyRead every message first, hide any you choose, then share the reel.

questions that gather good memories.

  • “Share one small moment you still think about.”
  • “What did they teach you that you still use?”
  • “Where do you feel them now?”
  • “What would you want their family to know about them?”

with care.

  • Memorial cards use the Pressed Flower look, quiet piano, and never party themes.
  • Written memories are welcome. Some people would rather write than speak.
  • Set a last day before the service so it’s ready in time.

questions people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Churpie is free for people, always: no ads, no per-card fees, no trial that runs out.

Can we play it at the funeral or memorial service?

Yes. The reel plays on any screen. Download it ahead of time so it doesn’t depend on the room’s internet.

Who sees the messages before the family does?

Only you. Every message lands on your private card page first, and you decide what goes in.

How long can the family keep it?

The reel is theirs to save and keep. We delete our copy, and every message, 14 days after the reel is made.
