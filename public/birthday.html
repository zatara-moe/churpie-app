how it works.

  1. Ask one questionTheir name, and the one thing you’d love everyone to say. About a minute.
  2. Send one linkTo the family group chat, WhatsApp or email. Everyone taps, records, done. No sign-in needed.
  3. Hand them the reelWatch messages arrive, hide any you like, then send them one link on the day.

questions that make great birthday videos.

  • “One memory, one wish. Say their name first.”
  • “What’s the funniest thing you’ve done together?”
  • “What do you hope this year brings them?”
  • “Tell them what you saw them do this year that made you proud.”

Big birthday? 30th, 50th, 60th, 75th: ask everyone for the year they met.

make it land.

  • Start a week early. Most people add theirs in the first two days, then a few stragglers.
  • Set a last day so nobody misses the moment.
  • For a kid’s party, pick the Confetti Pop card. For a milestone, pick Milestone Gold.
  • Play it at the party: put the reel on the TV and watch their face.

questions people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Churpie is free for people, always: no ads, no per-card fees, no trial that runs out.

How many people can be in a birthday video?

Up to 50. Each one adds about 15 seconds, or 30 if you choose.

Can grandparents do it?

Yes. They tap the link and press Record, or use their phone’s own camera, or write a short note instead. Nothing to download.

How long can they keep it?

The reel is theirs to save and keep. We delete our copy, and every message, 14 days after the reel is made.
