a send-off from the whole team.

Thirty-four years of shifts, one link. Drop it in the team chat, and everyone records 15 seconds from their phone or laptop: the people down the hall and the ones who moved away years ago. We stitch it into one reel for their last day.

how it works.

  1. Ask one questionTheir name and one thing everyone answers. About a minute.
  2. Post one linkIn Slack, Teams, email or the break-room QR code. No app, no accounts, no budget request.
  3. Play it on the last dayWatch messages come in, hide any you like, then share the reel at the send-off.

questions that make great send-off videos.

  • “The one thing you’ll miss about working with them.”
  • “Tell them about the day they saved you.”
  • “What should they do first with all that free time?”
  • “Finish this sentence: because of you, I...”

make it land.

  • Give people a week and set a last day. Work weeks fill up fast.
  • Use the QR code at a team meeting: everyone records in one go.
  • Ask the people who left the team years ago. They’re usually the most moving.
  • Play it on the big screen at the farewell, then send them the link to keep.

questions people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Churpie is free for people, always: no ads, no per-card fees, no trial that runs out.

Do coworkers need to sign up or install anything?

No. They tap the link and record in their browser, or write a note. Nobody makes an account, including you.

Can a whole department take part?

Up to 50 people per card. For a bigger group, start a second card for the same person.

Is it okay for work?

Every message lands on your private card page first, so you can watch each one and hide anything before the reel is made.
